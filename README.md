# Tally Ballatro
![Tally Ballatro mod cover image](https://i.imgur.com/59nyOqa.png)\
A Balatro mod about everyone's favorite band, Tally Hall! This is a moderately sized content mod that adds new jokers, tarots, spectrals, and enhancements which reference different aspects of everyone's favorite indie band, Tally Hall!

## Newest version: 1.5.0 - The Reunion Update!

The Reunion update is a celebration of Tally Hall's recent reunion. It adds several new jokers, overhauls visuals, and reworks a couple of cards. There are also some bugfixes.

### Full changelog for 1.4.0:

New content:
 - Several new jokers, including one new legendary
 - A new tie seal

Bugfixes and balancing:
 - Hiatus no longer permanently debuffs cards.
   - Instead, Hiatus debuffs cards for 15 years, 2 months, and 3 days (the length of Tally Hall's hiatus from 2011 to now)
 - Bananaman now carries over its mult when closing and reopening the game
 - Joe Hawley Joe Hawley properly retriggers cards played in the previous round
 - 2x Consumables textures are sized properly now

Visuals:
 - Zirconium edition touched up slightly
 - Several jokers got visual overhauls
   - The legendaries corresponding to the modern Tally Hall band members were updated visually to reflect their looks in 2026
 - Renamed Joe Hawley's legendary to The Banished (from The Singer)
 - Renamed Casey Shea's legendary to The Singer (from The Stand-In)
 - Updated the mod logo

## Content:

### New Jokers!
This mod adds 50 new jokers to the base game, ranging from common jokers like the English Chap, to legendaries which balatrify the main 5 guys in colorful ties you all know and love. Each joker has a unique and fun feature which may or may not be balanced... I'm not good at balancing lmao

### New consumables!
10 new comsumables come to Balatro, 5 spectrals and 5 tarots! Each tarot has its own unique feature, ranging from basic enhancers to even merging two cards together. Each spectral comes with its own powerful effect which could turn the tide of your game.

### Ties
In the Tie Update™, 6 new seals (referred to as "Ties") were added to the game, each designed to provide a minor synergy with their respective legendary joker!  
 - Red Tie: Joe Hawley
 - Blue Tie: Zubin Segishi
 - Green Tie: Andrew Horowitz
 - Yellow Tie: Rob Cantor
 - Gray Tie: Ross Federman
 - Orange Tie: Bora Karaca

An additional tie was added for Matthew Altruda as part of the Reunion update, being the pink tie.

### Enhancements
Tally Ballatro adds two new enhancements to Balatro: the Mechanical Card and the Blossom card! You can find out what these enhancements do in game.

### Edition
The Zirconium edition, based off of the song "Ruler of Everything," Tally Hall's 14th song in Marvin's Marvelous Mechanical Museum, is a basic xChips edition, working similarly to the Polychrome edition.
